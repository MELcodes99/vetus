Scammers take billions from older adults every year with fake emergencies, fake refunds and fake bills. Vetus checks every payment and every suspicious message first, blocks the scams, and asks the family when something looks off.

How families use it

01You set it upOpen the Vetus bot, tap "Protect a family member", and send your parent the invite link. Add the people they pay and set limits.
02They forward or payUnsure about a text or a call? They forward it and get a plain answer in seconds. Want to pay someone? Vetus checks it before any money moves.
03You decide the odd onesOrdinary bills go through. Scams are blocked. Anything unusual comes to you with Approve and Decline buttons and the full reasoning.

Three layers, strictest wins

Family rulesTrusted payees, per-payment and daily limits, and instant blocks for scam payment methods like gift cards and crypto ATMs.
SERV ReasoningChecks WHO, WHY, HOW, PRESSURE and FIT against known scam patterns, with a step-by-step trace the family can read. Scam text that tries to instruct the AI is flagged, not followed.
Kite Agent PassportApproved payments settle in USDC through Kite, which enforces the spending allowance on chain. Vetus checks the merchant's price before paying.

The AI can make a decision stricter, never softer. If the reasoning layer is down, Vetus holds the payment for a person.

For AI agents

People are starting to let AI assistants shop and pay for them, and those agents can be scammed too. Before your agent spends, ask Vetus:

curl -X POST https://YOUR-VETUS-URL/api/vet \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"payee":"Microsoft Refund Desk","amount":2.5,
       "memo":"Processing fee for refund",
       "context":"Your PC is infected. Pay the fee to get your $899 refund."}'

# → {"verdict":"BLOCK","pay":false,"reasoning":{"risk":96,"scamPattern":"tech support refund", ...}}
