Set up in two minutes

Everything happens in Telegram. Nobody has to install a new app or learn anything about crypto.

  1. You set it up

    Open the Vetus bot, tap Protect a family member, and send your parent their invite link. Add the people they pay and choose limits.

  2. They forward or pay

    Unsure about a text or a call? They forward it and get an answer in seconds. Paying someone? They tap /pay and Vetus checks it first.

  3. You decide the unusual ones

    Everyday bills go through. Scams are stopped. Anything unusual comes to you with Approve and Decline buttons and the reasons behind it.

Three checks before money moves

  • Your family's rules

    Trusted payees, a limit per payment and per day, and an instant stop on payment methods scammers love, like gift cards and crypto ATMs.

  • A careful second look

    SERV Reasoning checks who is asking, why, how they want to be paid, whether there's pressure, and whether it fits. Scam text that tries to instruct the AI gets flagged, not followed.

  • A locked wallet

    Approved payments are sent in digital dollars (USDC) through Kite Agent Passport, which caps spending no matter what.

The AI can make a decision stricter, never softer.

If the AI is unsure or unavailable, the payment waits for a person. Your rules always win.
